Gate & Weighbridge

Check every truck in, weigh it, and let it out only when it's done

Gatemen check trucks in against their transport order, and Scaling Operators record weights at the weighbridge. Each truck joins the Command Center queue the moment it's scaled in, and it can leave only once weighing and loading or unloading are complete.

What you get

A yard record you don't have to rewrite

Filled in from the order

Pick the transport order and the vehicle, driver and products appear. Nothing is copied from paper.

Weights in MT and kg

Weighbridge readings are entered in kilograms, shown in both units, and net weight is worked out for you.

No early exits

A truck can't gate out until it has been weighed out and its loading or unloading is finished.

Gate-in

Check every truck in at the gate

Gatemen record each arriving vehicle against the document it brings.

  • Pick a transport order and its vehicle, products, total weight and driver load automatically
  • Record the document type: supplier PO, dealer DO, distributor, port or internal transfer
  • Add the gate-in date and time and any remarks; a vehicle number is always required
  • See every visit still on site under Active Visits, and past ones in Visit History

On-demand orders

No order? Create one at the gate

When a dealer or supplier truck arrives with only a DO or PO, the gateman creates its transport order on the spot.

  • Dealer trucks: DO number, dealer from your dealer list, finished goods, quantity, vehicle and driver
  • Supplier trucks: PO number, supplier, raw material, quantity, vehicle and driver
  • The new order gets its own number and moves straight on to weighing, loading or unloading, and gate-out
  • Raw-material trucks go through quality control before they're unloaded

Scale-in

Weigh in and join the queue

Once a truck has gated in, the Scaling Operator records its first weight at the weighbridge.

  • Pick the transport order and enter the P.C. code from the yard's waiting slip
  • Enter the weighbridge reading in kilograms, and mark it if the vehicle came in empty
  • Print a queue slip with the P.C. code and a summary of the visit
  • The truck gets a queue token and joins the Command Center queue in the same step

Scale-out & gate-out

Weigh out, print the gate pass, close the visit

After loading or unloading, the truck comes back to the weighbridge before it leaves.

  • Record the second weight; net weight is calculated from the two readings
  • Print a gate pass with the vehicle, item, net weight and P.C. code
  • Weights are locked once scale-out is saved
  • Gate-out opens only after scale-out and finished loading or unloading, and for raw material only if QC hasn't failed
