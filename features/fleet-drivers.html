Know which trucks and drivers are ready to go

Keep every vehicle, driver and helper on one record. Driver Supervisors check vehicles before dispatch, send faulty ones to maintenance and keep attendance and leave up to date, so planners only see trucks and drivers that can actually run.

What you get

A fleet you can plan on

One record per vehicle

Registration, capacity, documents and GPS device in one place, with no duplicate registration or chassis numbers.

Checked before dispatch

Run the daily checklist or skip it. A vehicle that fails goes to maintenance and its order goes back to planning.

Only available drivers offered

Drivers on leave or already on an order are kept out of new assignments automatically.

Vehicles

Register every vehicle once

Admins keep the whole fleet on one list that planners and supervisors work from.

  • Registration number in the Bangladesh format, chassis number, type, body, fuel, capacity and wheels
  • Brand from your own brand list, plus model, GPS device and SIM number
  • Fitness certificate and insurance expiry dates on every vehicle
  • Add vehicles one by one or by CSV upload, then search and filter by type, brand, status or site

Dispatch check

Check the vehicle before it leaves

Once an own-fleet order has its vehicle, driver and schedule, it reaches the Driver Supervisor's dispatch queue.

  • Skip the checklist, or run the daily checklist across seven sections from engine to cabin
  • Score each item OK, Issue or N/A, with remarks
  • Vehicle OK releases the order for dispatch
  • Vehicle not OK sends the vehicle to maintenance and the order back to the planner for a new vehicle and crew

Maintenance

Bring vehicles back into service safely

Vehicles that fail inspection wait in the supervisor's maintenance queue.

  • See each vehicle's registration, the date it was flagged and its last inspection
  • Re-inspect with the same checklist once repairs are done
  • A pass makes the vehicle available for new orders; a fail keeps it in maintenance with a reason
  • Vehicles under maintenance never appear when orders are being assigned

Drivers & helpers

Keep drivers, attendance and leave in one place

Drivers and helpers have their own profiles, and the Driver Supervisor keeps their availability up to date.

  • Driver profiles with license number, type and expiry, plus experience, biodata and medical documents
  • Each driver gets a user account to see their own orders; helpers are added with their contact and references
  • Record daily attendance with entry and exit times; working hours are calculated and flagged above your daily limit
  • Leave sets the driver to On leave, and they can't be put on an order during it
