What you get

One place for every stock request

Finished-goods requisitions

Depot and feed mill officers request several products in one requisition, for the date and urgency they need.

Port shipments

Commercial officers register LC imports with the BL, vessel, containers and arrival notice in one record.

Capacity before you submit

See the destination's free space and projected use while you plan, so a request never surprises the site receiving it.

Product lines

Enter an item code, the rest fills itself in

Every line is taken from your product master, so names, units and weights are the same across sites and teams.

  • Name, category, sub-category, packing and unit of measure filled from the item code
  • Total weight calculated from quantity and weight per unit
  • Urgency set to Normal, High or Critical
  • Destination site and preferred delivery date and time on every requisition

Port shipments

Document each import once

The commercial team records the shipment as soon as the LC is open. Procurement plans the onward transport from the same record.

  • BL and LC numbers, vessel, voyage, ETA, C&F agent and point of loading
  • Container numbers kept as a simple list, one per row
  • Raw-material cargo lines with the arrival notice and other documents attached
  • Procurement sees every submitted shipment, read-only, and creates transport requests from it

Site capacity

Know the destination can take it

When you pick the destination, Optilogist shows how full that site is and how full it will be once this load arrives.

  • Total capacity, current stock and free capacity, in metric tons
  • Projected use after this requisition
  • A warning above the site's threshold, 80% by default and adjustable per site
  • For information only: the warning never blocks a requisition

Status & handover

From draft to transport planning, without chasing

Everyone can see where a requisition stands, and Distribution picks it up as soon as it is submitted.

  • Save as a draft and submit when ready; a submitted requisition is locked, except its urgency
  • It moves to In progress when Distribution creates the first transport request
  • Split into several transport requests, never more than was requested
  • Cancel with a reason, as long as no transport request is active
