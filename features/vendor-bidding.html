Vendor Bidding

Get competitive rates from approved vendors

When an order is planned on vendor fleet, the Transport Distribution Officer gives it to a contract vendor at the agreed rate or opens it for bids. Approved vendors bid from the vendor app within a set window, and you compare the bids and award each order to one vendor.

What you get

A fair, recorded way to hire vendor trucks

Contract or bid, per order

Give an order to a contract vendor at the agreed rate, or open it for bids when no contract applies.

A set bidding window

Every bid has an open and a close time. Bidding closes on its own at the deadline, and late bids aren't accepted.

Every award on record

Who published, who bid and who awarded the order is kept with the time of each step.

Vendors

Work only with vendors you've approved

Vendors register in the vendor app and ask to join your workspace.

  • Vendors sign up with their company details and upload their trade licence, BIN and TIN
  • Admins review each join request and approve it, or reject it with remarks
  • On approval, the admin sets the vendor as Contractual (fixed rate) or Non-Contractual (bidding)
  • Vendors see your orders and bids only after approval; planning teams can view the vendor list

Publish

Open orders for bids in a few steps

The Transport Distribution Officer (Vendor Fleet) publishes a bid for one transport order, or for several from the same transport request.

  • Set the number of vehicles, vehicle type and capacity you need
  • Add an optional reference rate as a guide for vendors; it isn't binding
  • Choose when bidding opens and closes, and move the window later if plans change
  • Follow every bid in Bids management, and cancel one with a reason if it's no longer needed

Vendor app

Vendors bid from their phone

Approved non-contractual vendors see open bids in the vendor app.

  • See the route, load, vehicle needs and closing time for each bid
  • Submit a rate and the number of vehicles they can provide
  • Each bid is time-stamped when it's submitted and can't be changed afterwards
  • Follow their bids as Open, Submitted or Won

Award

Compare bids and award the order

Once bidding closes, the officer compares the bids for each transport order.

  • See each vendor's proposed cost and submission time side by side
  • Award one vendor per transport order; on a bid for several orders, each order is awarded on its own
  • The winning vendor adds the truck, driver and helper details before the trip starts
  • The order then moves on to dispatch, and the vendor follows it in the vendor app
