Security & control

Your data stays yours

Each organization runs in its own isolated workspace, with access rules enforced on every screen and every request.

Isolated workspaces

A separate database schema and login address for every organization.

Two-step sign-in

One-time codes by email or SMS for admins and invited users.

Role-based access

Menus, buttons, data and API calls follow the user's role and site.

Approval rules

Nobody approves their own request, and approved requests are locked.

Audit trail

Who did what, as which role, with old and new values. Records can't be edited.

Clean master data

Only active vehicles, drivers and sites appear in dropdowns; records in use can't be deleted.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Optilogist replace our ERP or warehouse system?

No. Optilogist manages transport, yard and QC operations. It tracks total capacity per site, not bin-level inventory, and works alongside your ERP.

Do we need to buy GPS devices from you?

No. Optilogist connects to your existing GPS or vehicle tracking provider and matches each vehicle by its device ID.

Can we bring our existing vehicles, drivers and sites?

Yes. Every master list (vehicles, drivers, helpers, sites, products, dealers, suppliers) can be uploaded by CSV using a ready-made template.

How do our transport vendors join?

Vendors register in the Optilogist vendor app and request to join your organization. You approve them as contractual or bidding vendors.

How do drivers and yard staff get access?

Your admin invites them by email or phone. They receive a link and a one-time code, then set their own password.

Is the app available in Bangla?

This website is available in Bangla today. Bangla in the web portal and mobile apps is coming soon.
